Frequently Asked Questions

What time is it right now around the world? +
Our world clock updates every second, showing exact local time in 50+ major cities across all continents and time zones. Each card shows the live time, date, and whether it's currently day or night there.
How many time zones are there in the world? +
There are 24 standard time zones, but because some countries use 30-minute or 45-minute offsets, the real count is around 38–40 distinct UTC offsets in active use.
What is the difference between GMT and UTC? +
Both GMT and UTC show the same time at this moment. UTC is the scientific atomic-clock standard used to keep global time; GMT is a historical time zone centred on the Royal Observatory in Greenwich, England. For everyday use they are interchangeable.
Which country has the most time zones? +
France has the most time zones of any country — 12 in total — thanks to its overseas territories spread across the globe. If you count only contiguous land, Russia spans 11 time zones, more than any other nation.
Does China only have one time zone? +
Yes. Despite its enormous east-to-west span, all of China officially uses a single time zone: China Standard Time (CST), which is UTC+8. This means sunrise and sunset times can differ by several hours between eastern and western parts of the country.
